Ordering policies

Clear, fair service for every Mambo ya Tanga customer.

Delivery

Delivery fees are shown before checkout and depend on the selected Mafinga delivery zone. Please provide a complete address and phone number.

Preparation

Pickup and delivery times are estimates. Catering and pan orders may require advance notice; our team will call to confirm availability.

Changes and cancellations

Contact us as soon as possible using 0764 830 663. Once preparation has started, cancellation or refund eligibility may be limited.

Payments

Cash, Mobile Money and Card are accepted where available. Payment status is confirmed by Mambo ya Tanga staff.

Privacy and allergies

We use customer details only to process orders, provide support and communicate service updates. Tell us about allergies or dietary requirements in the order notes; customers should confirm ingredients with staff before ordering.

Questions? Contact Mambo ya Tanga in Mafinga, Iringa.
